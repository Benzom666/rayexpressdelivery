    What do the statuses mean?

    Order received — we have your booking. Picked up — the parcel left the sender. In transit — moving between hubs. Out for delivery — on the courier’s route today. Delivered — handed over, with photo proof.

    Where is my tracking number?

    It starts with RX- and arrives by email or SMS the moment your parcel is booked — usually within minutes of checkout or handover.

    My parcel says “at depot”. What now?

    It’s sorted and waiting for its route — typically out the next morning run. If it sits longer than 24 hours, contact us and we’ll chase it personally.

    Can I change the delivery address?

    Yes — up until the “out for delivery” scan. Use the link in your tracking SMS or call support and we’ll redirect it mid-route where possible.

    Still stuck?

    Send your tracking number and we’ll reply with a human answer, not a status code.
